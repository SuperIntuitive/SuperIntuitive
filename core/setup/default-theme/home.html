01 / GETTING STARTED

Start with a page. Make it yours.

You do not need to plan an entire site before you begin. Start with a few sections, put your content in place, and refine the design as you go.

  1. 01

    Shape the structure

    Add a page and arrange its blocks. Give each section a clear purpose.

  2. 02

    Bring it to life

    Add your words and images, then adjust the spacing, color, and type.

  3. 03

    Review and publish

    Check your changes in test before you move them to your live site.

This page is your starting point. Sign in with your administrator account to open the editing tools and start changing it.

02 / VISUAL EDITING

Work where the website happens.

Select an element on the page and adjust it in place. Shape the layout, edit the content, and see how the pieces fit together as you work.

03 / REUSABLE BLOCKS

A few good pieces go a long way.

Build with sections you can understand and reuse. A header, a navigation menu, a content area, a footer: each block has its own markup, styles, and behavior.

Building blockWhat it does
PagesGive each destination a name, a path, and a place in your site.
BlocksOrganize the sections that make up a page, and reuse them across the site.
StylesControl how each element looks, from type and color to spacing and layout.
ScriptsAdd behavior when a page needs to do more than display content.

04 / CONTENT & MEDIA

Give your content a home.

Keep the words, images, and supporting files close to the pages that use them. Organize your media, update your content, and build a site that has room to grow.

CONTENT

MEDIA

05 / STYLING & LAYOUT

The small decisions make it yours.

Use visual styling controls to adjust selectors and properties. Fine-tune the space around an element, change the typography, and give your site a consistent visual language.
